# Timeline

### POLITE, int8 inference accelerator (Rev Silicon)
date: 2026-09-21
period: Sep 2026 to present
cover: projects/polite/chip.png
link: projects/polite

Joined Rev Silicon, the Texas A&M first-chip tapeout effort. I am designing POLITE, a 4x4 int8 matrix unit for Tiny Tapeout, with a cycle-accurate C golden model and matching SystemVerilog RTL. The pad-clock target is 66 MHz (15.15 ns) for STA and the pin budget, not a measured clock. At that target the chip takes 2 bytes in per beat, 132000000 bytes/s.

### IMM Research tooling
date: 2025-05-08
period: May 2025 to Aug 2025
cover: timeline/thumbs/imm-research-tooling.png
link: none

Foreclosure lists and deed records are scraped and matched on cause number. The post-deed pipeline checks CAD and runs financials, and a queue worker downloads Re:SearchTX court documents. Opening a case only prefills fields and attaches those files. AI proposes document buckets for a person to review before extraction, and defendant names are copied onto the tree without contact details. Skip-trace dumps are uploaded by the researcher. Comparator, the later platform, is a separate entry.
