Projects

  1. POLITEA pad-fed 4×4 int8 matrix tile for Tiny Tapeout: 16 PEs with 2 MACs each, single-edge clocking, a cycle-accurate C golden model and matching Verilog RTL. The 66 MHz (15.15 ns) clock is an STA and pin-budget target, not a measured clock. Results leave at one byte every 2 clocks to respect the 33 MHz pad-out rating.
  2. ComparatorA web UI and API for property and court research. Development runs on my tower machine. Production deploys a committed git SHA.
  3. GenDiffOngoing researchOngoing exploratory research with Prof. Krishna Narayanan, January through May 2026. GenDiff transmits a prompt, a seed, and a diff. Not a finished result.
  4. splendid-hopperA small C++20 Vulkan engine: ASCII-filtered roguelike prototype plus an animated torus live-wallpaper.
  5. study_lensA minimal drawing app with no LLM.
  6. Aggie SchedulerA Qt6 desktop app for classes, descriptions, and prerequisites.
  7. LucidScanLucidScan is a Chrome extension prototype, grown from a TIDALHack 25 project, that adds local-score badges to images and selected text on sites you enable. It runs open models (TMR text detector, CLIP ViT-B/32) entirely in your browser; the score is a signal, not an authenticity verdict.
  8. ECEN 350 CPUA teaching single-cycle datapath in Verilog: fetch, decode, ALU, register file, and data memory.
  9. JBL BLEDecompiling the JBL app, looking for unused functionality, and reverse engineering the communication protocol.
