Electrical Engineering student at Texas A&M, engineering honors, math minor, graduating May 2028. I work across semiconductor hardware and production software: architecture for POLITE, an int8 inference accelerator aimed at Tiny Tapeout with Rev Silicon / Reveille Silicon; a Summer 2026 Product Operations internship at Samsung Austin Semiconductor focused on line-down diagnosis and alert routing; and exploratory LLM compression research with Prof. Krishna Narayanan (llmzip extensions and GenDiff — ongoing).

I built the production legal-research stack for IMM Research, my dad’s Austin tax-sale business: county scrapers (deeds, foreclosure lists, CAD, court), LLM extraction, and a SvelteKit research UI (Comparator) used on live Texas cases. The path there started with TI-84 CE assembly in January 2024, then Ignite hardware, hackathons (Build4Good, LucidScan), Lunabotics CDH, a Qt course scheduler, a Vulkan engine, and Verilog CPUs — with leadership on A&M’s first-chip tapeout org.
